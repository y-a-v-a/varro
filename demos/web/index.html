Every word shaded by how surprised the model was to see it. It reports improbability, not error — proper names and rare words light up exactly the way OCR damage does. A hot word is a place to look, never a verdict.

The same tokens scored four times, changing only the era tag in front of them. Margins are small by construction — a tag is two tokens against a few hundred — so the answer comes with the accuracy actually measured on held-out text of known date.
