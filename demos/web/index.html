Mark a hole with [...]. The model proposes supplements reading forward, then every candidate is re-scored across the text after the gap — which is what a left-to-right model cannot do by generating. The two columns are that difference, and the table under them shows, token by token, where the text after the gap made up its mind.

Every word shaded by how surprised the model was to see it. It reports improbability, not error — proper names and rare words light up exactly the way OCR damage does. A hot word is a place to look, never a verdict.

The same tokens scored four times, changing only the era tag in front of them. Margins are small by construction — a tag is two tokens against a few hundred — so the answer comes with the accuracy actually measured on held-out text of known date.

Two readings that differ in one thing. The margin, in bits, is the model's judgement about that one thing, and the token table says where the cost was paid — on the changed form, or on the text after it that the wrong form made harder to predict. The built-in suite runs two dozen such pairs across agreement, case, mood and voice, and keeps the ones the model gets wrong.
