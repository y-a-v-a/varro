All Gaul is divided into three parts, one of which the Belgae inhabit, the
Aquitani another, and the third those who in their own language are called
Celts and in ours Gauls. All these differ from one another in language,
institutions and laws. The river Garonne separates the Gauls from the
Aquitani, the Marne and the Seine separate them from the Belgae. Of all these
the Belgae are the bravest, because they are furthest from the culture and
refinement of the Province, and merchants least often travel to them and
import the things that tend to soften the spirit.
